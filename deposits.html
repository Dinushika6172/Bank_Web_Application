Deposits

Trustfund Bank offers various deposit products to help you manage and grow your savings.

Types of Deposits

  • Fixed Deposits
  • Recurring Deposits
  • Certificates of Deposit (CDs)

Fixed Deposits

    A fixed deposit account with a guaranteed return on investment over a specified period.

    Interest Rates: 3% to 5% APY.
    Minimum Deposit: $500.
    Term Length: 6 months to 5 years.

Recurring Deposits

    An account that allows you to make regular deposits over a specified period.


    Interest Rates: 2.5% to 4% APY.
    Minimum Deposit: $100.
    Term Length: 1 year to 10 years.

Certificates of Deposit (CDs)

    A deposit account with a fixed term and interest rate.


    Interest Rates: 3% to 6% APY.
    Minimum Deposit: $1,000.
    Term Length: 3 months to 5 years.

Features

  • Guaranteed returns
  • Flexible terms
  • Secure investment
