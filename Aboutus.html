About Us

Welcome to Trustfund Bank of Sri Lanka (TFBS), a leading financial institution committed to providing reliable banking services to individuals, businesses, and communities across Sri Lanka. Founded with the vision of fostering financial inclusion and stability, TFBS has become synonymous with trust, innovation, and excellence in the banking sector.

Our Mission

At TFBS, our mission is to empower our customers by offering secure, innovative, and accessible financial solutions. We aim to create value for all our stakeholders, drive economic growth, and contribute to the sustainable development of our society.

Our Vision

Our vision is to be the most trusted and preferred bank in Sri Lanka, known for our customer-centric approach, cutting-edge technology, and commitment to ethical business practices.

Our Values

  • Innovation: We embrace change and encourage innovation to better serve our customers.
